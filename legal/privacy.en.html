Privacy Policy

Athlete Diary: the Telegram bot @lightweight_log_bot and the iPhone app. Effective September 25, 2026.

Straight to the point: what the service stores about you, why, who it is shared with and when, and how to delete all of it. The bot and the app are one service with one account and one database, so they share one policy.

What is stored

  • Account. If you came through Telegram — your Telegram ID and username. If you signed in to the app with Sign in with Apple — the identifier Apple issues for this app, and your e-mail if Apple shared it (it is only sent on the first sign-in and may be an Apple private relay address). If you linked Telegram to an app account — your Telegram ID as well.
  • Settings. Language, time zone, weight units, e1RM formula, which notifications and hints are on, calorie goal.
  • Training log. Workouts, exercises, sets (weight, reps, RPE), workout and exercise notes, programs, records, achievements and ranks.
  • Body weight and food diary. Weight entries; what you ate and the estimated calories and macros.
  • Training profile. Experience, goal, training days per week, equipment and limitations. You write the limitations yourself, and they may include health information — an injury, for example. Only write what you are comfortable sharing with a coach.
  • AI coach chat. Your questions, the answers, and whatever the coach logged at your request.
  • Photos and video. Your own exercise photos are stored on the server. A photo you send the coach in the app is kept in the chat history. A set video is not stored in full: after the review, a single preview frame is kept for the chat history. Voice messages are sent for speech recognition; only the recognized text is saved.
  • Notifications. The Apple Push Notification service (APNs) device token, to send push notifications to your iPhone.
  • Service data. Access tokens for the app and for AI clients you connected (MCP); an activity log (which buttons were tapped and what was typed); the cost of AI requests; the link tag you arrived through and who invited you; share links you created; if you supported the project with Telegram Stars — the payment ID and amount.

The service does not collect your location, contacts, advertising identifiers or Apple Health data.

Why

To keep your training log: record sets, calculate records, volume and progress, show your history in the bot and the app, build programs and answer questions with the AI coach, and send the reminders you haven't turned off. The activity log and cost records are used to find bugs, see where things are awkward, and keep AI spending under control.

Your data is not sold, the service shows no ads, and your data is not used for advertising or for tracking you in other apps.

Who it is shared with

Only with the services a feature cannot work without, and only as much as that feature needs:

  • xAI (Grok model) — the AI coach. It receives your question and the log data needed to answer it (workouts, weight, profile, food), a photo if you sent one, and a food description or photo to estimate calories. It also writes the post-workout comment (if you turned it on) and the weekly summary, and searches the web for some questions. Once a day xAI also receives a summary of that day's activity log (with username or ID), which the service owner uses to see where people get stuck.
  • Novita AI (Qwen3-VL model) — technique review: receives the set video you sent for review.
  • OpenAI — speech recognition: receives your voice message when you log a set or ask a question by voice.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and push delivery (APNs).
  • Telegram — if you use the bot, your conversation with it goes through Telegram. Feedback from the bot and the app is delivered to the service owner on Telegram.
  • Fly.io — hosting: the server and database run in a Fly.io data center in Frankfurt, Germany. Tigris — storage for the database backup.
  • AI clients you connect yourself (for example Claude or ChatGPT via MCP) get access to your log only after you grant it. You can revoke that access.

These services operate in different countries, so your data may be processed outside your own.

A share link is a snapshot of a program, a day or an exercise. Anyone with the link can see it. The link lives for 180 days, and you can revoke it sooner.

How long it is kept

  • Your log, settings and photos — as long as the account exists.
  • Activity log — 30 days; AI cost records — 90 days.
  • Past AI coach conversations in the app — 180 days; the current conversation — until you start a new one.

Backups. The database is continuously copied to Tigris storage; once a day a separate copy is made — the last several are kept on the server, and the same copy is sent to the service owner on Telegram. The host also takes disk snapshots and keeps them for a few days. Backups exist only to recover from failures. After you delete your account, your data remains in them until they are replaced by newer ones, and in the owner's Telegram until they are deleted there.

How to delete your account

  • In the app: Settings → “🗑 Delete account”.
  • In the bot: “🔧 Settings” → “🗑 Delete account”.

Deletion immediately erases everything from the database: workouts, sets, records, food, weight, programs, AI coach chat, tokens and the Apple ID link — and removes your exercise photos and AI chat photos from disk. There is one account for the bot and the app: delete it in one place and it is gone in both. Deletion cannot be undone. To keep your history, export it first with “📤 Export CSV”.

Signing out of the app deletes nothing — it only unlinks that device.

Age

The service is meant for adults and is not intended for children. If you are under 16, use it only with the consent of a parent or guardian. The service does not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if such an account comes to light, it will be deleted.

Your rights

You can view your data in the bot and the app, export your history to CSV, correct entries, revoke AI client access, turn off push notifications and delete your account entirely. Need something else — write in.

Contact

  • In the app: Settings → “Feedback”.
  • In the bot @lightweight_log_bot: the /feedback command.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will be announced in the bot or the app.
